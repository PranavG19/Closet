Atelier — Baseline

The current Atelier design at its best — editorial restraint with warm cream canvas, sunken garment wells, serif display headings, hairline dividers, and a single earned crimson action per screen.

Signature move: Every garment is a clean CSS-drawn silhouette on a 6px sunken well, tinted per-piece via one custom property so the shapes read as white/black/camel/rose — no images, fully consistent.

Atelier — Ivory Air

Quiet luxury turned COOL: the warm cream is flipped to a crisp grey-blue gallery canvas, the grid drops to one tall roomy column with ~50% more air, and every divider is a true 0.5px hairline. Owns the temperature-and-space lever.

Signature move: A clean warm-to-cool FLIP — the whole canvas shifts to cool grey-blue and the layout opens to a single airy column, so at a thumbnail it reads unmistakably as 'the calm, cool one' against baseline's warm density.

Atelier — Editorial Bold

The Atelier system pushed to full magazine-masthead scale — a 70px Didone display headline hung hard-left over an indented body column, wrapping to two lines tight to the frame. Loud, confident, asymmetric. Owns the type-scale lever.

Signature move: A high-contrast Didone masthead so large it dwarfs the body and breaks to two lines ('The Closet', 'Camel outerwear', 'About You') — a different typographic species from baseline, reading as a fashion cover at a glance.

Atelier — Serif Throughout

A wardrobe read like a book: set ENTIRELY in Georgia — the uppercase sans overlines are retired for lowercase serif-italic labels, the tab bar becomes serif-italic wordmarks, and an oversized crimson drop-cap opens the Today title. Owns the all-serif/italic lever.

Signature move: Sans is gone from every corner including the navigation, italics carry the intimate voice, and a big crimson serif drop-cap on 'Camel outerwear' makes the literary/bespoke metaphor unmistakable.

20% · a step away

Editorial Noir

The Atelier editorial system inverted into a warm-ink nighttime atelier — bone-cream type and the same serif display on deep charcoal, garment cutouts floating on darker wells lit by a soft spot, with the crimson action now glowing.

Signature move: The crimson accent literally emits light — every earned action carries a soft crimson glow, so the one accent reads as the only warm light source in a dark room.

20% · a step away

Lookbook (imagery-forward)

The 'app' layout language traded for a printed lookbook: full-bleed edge-to-edge garment tiles with type set over the imagery, oversized magazine 01–04 number tags, Today as a full-screen cover, and a numbered dark editorial header on You. Owns the imagery-forward lever.

Signature move: Edge-to-edge full-bleed tiles with big serialised '01–04' tags and name/state set directly over each garment on a gradient scrim — card chrome dropped entirely so the closet reads as a printed spread, not a grid of cards.

10% · a real departure

Studio Pop

A joyful Gen-Z boutique departure that trades cream-and-crimson restraint for confident colour-blocked panels (electric grape, hot tangerine, zesty lime), oversized italic display type, and rounder shapes — loud in palette but still minimalist in composition.

Signature move: Each tile sits on its own full-saturation candy-tinted well and the Today reveal becomes a radial tangerine-to-magenta hero with floating blend-mode blobs — colour-blocking promoted from accent to structural surface.

Atelier — Linen & Sage

Botanical Atelier: a warm oatmeal-linen canvas with sage carried across chips, tabs, status dots and a soft sage wash behind the hero (not just one button), and destruction quarantined to a warm terracotta. Owns the palette-swap lever.

Signature move: Sage becomes the whole screen's voice — active chip, tabs, dots, FAB and a hero wash — while the destructive Delete is the one clay-terracotta note, so the palette shift owns real estate instead of a single accent dot.

Atelier — Tactile Cards

A depth shift on the Atelier system: a darker warm-greige canvas so white paper cards float as distinct slabs with deep soft shadows and larger radii, and the Today hero garment sits on a raised circular pedestal. Owns the elevation/materiality lever.

Signature move: Warm layered elevation replaces hairlines everywhere — garments and account sections become raised paper cards over a recessed greige ground, and the Today hero rests on a real plinth like an object on display.
