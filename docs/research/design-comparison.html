The slop tells → the craft fix
Composition
A giant empty well dominates the screen with one word in it.
Lead with a full-bleed garment image; the product is the hero, not a placeholder.
Everything's a card
Uniform 28px rounded card + shadow on every element → component-demo look.
Mix modes: full-bleed hero, edge-to-edge grid, hairline dividers. Cards earn their place.
The loud CTA
A full-width shouting pink pill ("I wore this").
A quiet, confident action — underlined caps, or an icon. Premium is restrained.
Dead-centre everything
Centred title, centred word, centred button — no tension.
Left-align, asymmetry, a real baseline grid. Editorial, not PowerPoint.
No type hierarchy
One sans weight for everything; nothing feels authored.
Serif display headline + tiny uppercase overlines + restrained body. Contrast in size & style.
Placeholder words
"top", "outerwear" printed where a photo belongs → looks unfinished.
Always render the cutout; if none yet, a subtle branded state, never a category label.
Nav clutter
Seven tabs crammed edge to edge.
Five: Today · Closet · Add · Outfits · You. Plan folds into Today; Account into "You".
Timid spacing
Even padding everywhere; no rhythm.
Intentional rhythm — generous around the hero, tight in the grid. Space is a tool.

Note: every colour here is the palette already shipping (cream #F6F2EC, camel wells, the state dots) plus a serif for display — the crafted side changes layout and hierarchy, not the theme. This is a static mockup for direction, not the running app.
