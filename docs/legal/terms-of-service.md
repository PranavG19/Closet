## 4. What the Service does — and what it is not

[App Name] screens photos on your device, then digitises the garment photos you approve into a
wardrobe, suggests outfits from what you have marked clean, tracks what you wore, and offers an
optional self-identified colour palette.

**All styling, outfit, colour-harmony and palette guidance is advisory. It is never prescriptive.**
To be specific about what that means:

- Suggestions are **ideas, not instructions**. Nothing in the Service prevents you from wearing
  anything, hides an item for being "wrong", or nags you about a choice.
- Colour-harmony guidance is a set of deterministic rules about how colours sit together. It is a
  convention, not a fact about you.
- The colour palette is built from **swatches you pick yourself**. It is not derived from a photo of
  you, and it is labelled beta.
- **The Service makes no assessment, claim or judgement about your appearance, your body, your
  attractiveness, your size or your shape.** It does not scan, measure or model your body. It has no
  opinion about how you look. It organises garments.
- Nothing in the Service is professional styling, medical, psychological, or health advice.

Garment attributes are produced automatically and will sometimes be wrong — a colour misread, a
category mislabelled, a garment missed or split. You can correct items in the app. We do not warrant
that the digitisation of your closet is complete or accurate.

Weather-aware suggestions rely on third-party weather data, which can be wrong or unavailable. When
weather is unavailable, suggestions still work, just without weather bias.
