## Before publishing — fix these (workflow self-critique, no high-severity defects)

- **[medium/keyword-cannibalization]** `premium-closet-app` — premium-closet-app and landing-page are two near-duplicate brand/product pages targeting the same transactional intent (premium/'digitize my closet' app) with the same pitch and an almost identical privacy section. Their targetKeyword + secondaryKeywords overlap heavily ('digitize your closet app' / 'digitize my closet app', 'outfit planner app', 'what to wear app'). Google will see them as competing duplicates and likely suppress one.
  - **Fix:** Differentiate the two clearly: make landing-page the conversion page (no organic ranking ambition, can be noindex or canonical to the article) and premium-closet-app the indexable SEO article, OR consolidate. Give each a distinct primary keyword and rewrite the shared privacy block so they aren't near-identical.
- **[medium/keyword-cannibalization]** `how-to-organize-your-wardrobe` — The pillar's 'Step 2 — Digitize your closet (three ways, and the privacy tradeoffs)' replicates the entire three-methods comparison (spreadsheet / photo-by-photo / camera-roll scan + privacy) that is the whole point of how-to-digitize-your-closet. A hub page should summarize and link, not reproduce the spoke, or the two compete for 'digitize my closet' intent. The pillar also lists 'digitize my closet', 'capsule wardrobe', and 'outfit planner' as secondaryKeywords — the exact targetKeywords of three spokes.
  - **Fix:** Cut Step 2 down to a 2-3 sentence primer that names the three methods and hands off to how-to-digitize-your-closet for the full comparison. Drop the spokes' head-terms from the pillar's secondaryKeywords list so each spoke owns its intent.
- **[medium/other]** `how-to-organize-your-wardrobe` — Internal-link URL prefixes are inconsistent across the set. The pillar and premium-closet-app link with no /blog prefix (e.g. /what-to-wear-nothing-to-wear, /how-to-digitize-your-closet), while what-to-wear, outfit-ideas, outfit-planner, and the canonicals use /blog/. Depending on routing, one convention yields 404s and breaks the whole hub-and-spoke link equity.
  - **Fix:** Pick one path convention (the canonicals imply /blog/) and normalize every internal link across all eight artifacts to match it.
- **[medium/other]** `outfit-planner-app-guide` — Mis-anchored internal link: '(If you want to go deeper on setup methods specifically, we compared them in [how to organize your wardrobe](/blog/how-to-organize-your-wardrobe).)' The deep three-way setup-methods comparison actually lives in how-to-digitize-your-closet, not the pillar. Readers land on the wrong page and the topical link signal points at the wrong target.
  - **Fix:** Repoint this link to /blog/how-to-digitize-your-closet.
- **[low/inclusivity]** `outfit-ideas-from-your-own-closet` — The 'rule of thirds' section leans prescriptive and toward an idealized body standard — 'avoid splitting yourself in half', 'flattens the whole line', 'raise the waistline and lengthen the leg'. This nudges toward a 'correct' proportion to achieve, which rubs against the 'advisory, never prescriptive / never body-shaming' voice invariant.
  - **Fix:** Reframe as taste/preference rather than correction: present proportion as one lever to change the look you get, not a flaw to fix. Drop 'lengthen the leg' / 'avoid splitting yourself in half' framing in favor of neutral ('shifting where the visual break falls changes the feel — try it and see what you like').
- **[low/overclaim]** `premium-closet-app` — The H1 'Digitizes Your Wardrobe in Seconds', the metaDescription, and the landing-page FAQ ('digitizes each garment... organizes them into a browsable wardrobe — in seconds') can read as the WHOLE closet being digitized in seconds. Per the product truth, only a small teaser (~5-10 items) is parsed in seconds for the reveal; the full camera-roll parse happens after payment. The premium body is careful ('a preview of your wardrobe'), but the headline/meta/FAQ overreach.
  - **Fix:** Make the 'in seconds' claim consistently refer to the preview/reveal moment (e.g. 'see a preview of your real closet in seconds'), and let the full-closet build be the post-unlock step, matching the teaser-then-full-parse flow. Apply the same edit to the landing-page FAQ.
- **[low/other]** `how-to-digitize-your-closet` — Canonical placeholder format is inconsistent with the rest of the set: this post uses a hardcoded 'https://REPLACE-WITH-CANONICAL-DOMAIN/blog/...' string, premium-closet-app uses 'https://[DOMAIN]/...', while the others use the '{{CANONICAL_URL}}' token. Inconsistent placeholders risk a real REPLACE/DOMAIN string shipping live if the publish step only substitutes one token form.
  - **Fix:** Standardize all canonicals on a single placeholder token ({{CANONICAL_URL}}) so the publish substitution is uniform and unmissable.
- **[low/overclaim]** `capsule-wardrobe-app-guide` — The post correctly points at the wear log (MVP) for 'which pieces are dead weight', but cost-per-wear, dead-stock, and wardrobe analytics screens are roadmap (deferred), not MVP. As the truncated 'what features quietly do the work' section continues, there's a risk of implying the app surfaces cost-per-wear / dead-stock analytics.
  - **Fix:** Keep the app's role limited to the MVP wear-log record ('a timestamped record of what you actually wore'); discuss cost-per-wear/dead-stock only as a concept the record enables, not a screen the app ships today. If a future hint is used, frame it clearly as coming-later (allowed at most once).

## Verdict
Solid, on-brand drafts overall — warm/confident voice holds, privacy on-device gate is described accurately everywhere (no privacy misstatements), no post promises try-on/shopping/social as shipping, and all metaDescriptions are within the 155-char limit. No high-severity defects. The real risks are structural rather than voice: (1) landing-page and premium-closet-app are near-duplicate product pages competing for the same intent, and the pillar duplicates the how-to-digitize three-methods comparison — both cannibalization risks; (2) internal links use inconsistent /blog vs no-prefix paths and one mis-anchored link, which will break the hub-and-spoke; (3) minor overclaim where 'digitized in seconds' reads as the whole closet rather than the teaser preview; (4) one low inclusivity nudge in the proportion/'lengthen the leg' styling advice to soften toward advisory. Fix the cannibalization pair, normalize link paths/canonicals, and trim the pillar's digitize section, then this set is publish-ready.
