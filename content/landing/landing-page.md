## FAQ

### How does the scan work?
You point the app at your camera roll. It finds the photos with your clothes in them, digitizes each garment into a clean cutout, and organizes them into a browsable wardrobe — in seconds, not an afternoon of photographing.

### Do I have to photograph every item?
No. That's the whole point. If you've taken photos wearing your clothes, we can build your closet from those. You can also import specific photos if you'd rather hand-pick.

### What actually leaves my phone?
Only the clothing photos you approve. We check everything on your device first and filter out anything that isn't a garment photo before a single image is uploaded.

### Does the app look at my body?
No. We never scan, measure, or analyze your body. Any color or palette guidance comes from a short quiz you fill in yourself — never from a photo of you.

### How does it know what to suggest?
It looks at what's currently clean in your closet, checks today's local weather, and pairs items using simple color-harmony rules. Suggestions are ideas, never rules — you're always in charge.

### What if my closet is a mess of laundry?
That's normal, and the app treats it that way. Mark things as in the wash or unavailable, and suggestions quietly skip them until they're back. One tap logs what you wore so it all stays accurate.

### Is there a free trial?
No. You'll see your digitized closet before you're asked to subscribe, so you can decide with your own eyes rather than on a promise.

### What's coming later?
We're starting with the everyday essentials: your closet, your outfits, your laundry. More is on the way — but everything on this page ships today.
